\paragraph{Additional related latent variable models.}
\label{paragraph:rebuttal_related_work}
Several LVMs target interpretability through objects other than individual sparse latents. Switching linear dynamical systems segment population activity into discrete dynamical regimes, each with its own linear dynamics~\citep{linderman_2017_rslds}. PSID and its nonlinear successor DPAD prioritize and dissociate the latent dynamics that are relevant to a measured behavior~\citep{sani_2021_psid, sani_2024_dpad}, and DFINE learns a nonlinear latent manifold with tractable dynamics that supports flexible inference~\citep{abbaspourazad_2024_dfine}. pi-VAE learns identifiable latent manifolds by conditioning on task variables~\citep{zhou_2020_pivae}. These methods interpret dynamical regimes or latent manifolds, and several use behavioral or task labels during training; NLDisco emphasizes individual, interpretable sparse features and their contributions to neural reconstruction, learned from neural activity alone. Multi-session and multi-subject transfer is a distinct challenge addressed by foundation models such as POYO and NDT2~\citep{azabou_2023_unified, ye_2023_ndt2}, which pool recordings to improve decoding; NLDisco's multi-session variant (\hyperref[subsubsection:rebuttal_multisession]{\ref*{subsubsection:rebuttal_multisession}}) instead targets the transfer of individual latent interpretations.
